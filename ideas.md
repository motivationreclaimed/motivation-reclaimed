# Ideas — SKETCH style, the Bear Cub, uplifting (use top first, delete when used, add new ones in the same spirit)
Each = one warm true message + a tiny wolf story (setback/doubt → small turn → hope). Hook card / final card in caps.

- YOU ARE ALLOWED TO START OVER — he erases a messy scribble wall, draws a clean line, walks on. "MESSED UP?" → "START OVER. IT'S ALLOWED."
- IT'S OKAY TO ASK FOR HELP — wolf can't lift a big block alone, a second sketched figure helps; "TOO HEAVY?" → "ASK FOR HELP."
- ONE DAY AT A TIME — calendar of 30 squares, he only looks at today's square, ticks it. "30 DAYS?" → "JUST TODAY."
- YOUR PACE IS FINE — he walks while a big clock spins fast above; the clock stops, sun comes out. "TICK TOCK." → "YOUR PACE IS FINE."
- THE SEED KNOWS — seed underground (cutaway line), roots grow before anything shows above. "NOTHING'S HAPPENING?" → "ROOTS FIRST."
- KIND WORDS TO YOURSELF — speech scribble "I'M USELESS" crumples, replaced by "I'M LEARNING". "WHAT YOU SAY" → "BECOMES WHAT YOU BELIEVE."
- THE LONG WAY ROUND — path blocked by a wall, he walks round a longer path, still reaches the sun. "BLOCKED?" → "THERE'S ANOTHER WAY."
- YOU'VE SURVIVED BEFORE — wide line of past "storms" (clouds behind him), each passed; he faces a new cloud calmly. "AGAIN?" → "YOU'VE DONE THIS BEFORE."
- LIGHT A CANDLE — dark, wolf strikes a small light (sketched flame), the dark shrinks around him. "TOO DARK?" → "START WITH ONE LIGHT."
- BEGIN BEFORE YOU'RE READY — he waits at a start line counting "3… 2…" forever, then just steps. "NOT READY?" → "BEGIN ANYWAY."
- MORNING AFTER — FLOOR_SIT at night, moon; morning sun rises, he stands and stretches (ARMS_UP). "LAST NIGHT" → "WAS NOT THE END."
- GROWING PAINS — wolf trips over his own growing shadow/size line, gets up taller. "IT HURTS?" → "YOU'RE GROWING."
